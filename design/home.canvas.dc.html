Why we're built different

Quality by design.

We're a small studio that bakes in batches, not an assembly line.

  • Small batches. Every tray is designed in-house, seasonal, and baked fresh. No assembly line.
  • Designed in Jupiter. Our own design team builds every piece for single-color TPU, tested on our own printers.
  • Our own studio. A fleet of 30 printers: 10 Bambu P1S, 10 Qidi Max 4 and 10 Prusa Core One, plus in-house compute.
  • Built to be used. Flexible TPU 95A. Washable, dishwasher safe, made for daily use.
  • Checked before it ships. Every piece is [[CONFIRM: hand-checked]] before it leaves the studio.
