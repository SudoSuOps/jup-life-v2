The studio details

Your privacy.

Effective October 22, 2026. Contact Dee with any questions about your information.

JUP LIFE Studio can receive information you provide when contacting us, requesting a sample or placing an order, including contact, shipping and order information. Sample and custom-order requests, including any logo file you choose to attach, are emailed to the studio through Resend, and the studio replies from its own inbox. Payments are handled through Stripe’s hosted checkout; this site does not collect your card details.

JUP LIFE Drops email list

If you join the Drops list, we keep your email address (and nothing else) to send you news about new batches and specials. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or write to hello@juplifestudio.com and we will remove you. Your address is held in Resend, our email provider, and is never sold.

How information is used

Information supports responding to inquiries, processing orders, delivering products and keeping the studio secure. Stripe processes checkout information under its own privacy terms, and customer contact and shipping details for orders are managed in Stripe. Cloudflare hosts the website. This site does not keep a database of sample requests; they live in the studio’s email.

Your browser

Your bag is saved locally on your device. This site uses no advertising pixels or visitor analytics. Hosting and payment services may process technical information for security and service delivery.

Questions and requests

Contact dee@juplifestudio.com for privacy questions or to request access to or deletion of information, subject to records needed for orders and applicable obligations. We retain information for as long as needed to respond to you, fulfill orders, handle returns and disputes, maintain security and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.
