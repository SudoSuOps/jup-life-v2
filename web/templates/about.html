Meet Mrs. Smash

A little piece of our happy place.

Dee is Mrs. Smash: mom of four, wife to one, and the heart of the studio. She and Smash bake every batch right here in Jupiter.

Mrs. Smash (Dee) is the face of JUP LIFE by Mrs. Smash, a Jupiter, FL 3D printing studio. The studio bakes 3D printed coasters, custom bar coasters and restaurant coasters.

The studio serves Jupiter, Tequesta, Juno Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County.
