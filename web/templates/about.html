The studio

Hi, I'm Dee — Mrs. Smash.

JUP LIFE is a coaster bakery in Jupiter, Florida. Every morning we load 30 printers — 10 Bambu P1S, 10 Qidi Max 4, 10 Prusa Core One — and bake washable TPU coasters in small seasonal batches. I check every piece myself. We started with Palm Beach County restaurants and bars, because a good coaster is the first thing your guest touches. Want your logo fresh baked? Your first sample is on us.
