Shipping

We offer free shipping on all U.S. orders, with no order minimum. Orders leave our Jupiter, Florida studio within 2–3 business days after purchase. This is processing and dispatch time; carrier transit time is additional and delivery dates are not guaranteed. Confirm your shipping address during checkout.

Returns and care

We accept returns of unused items within 30 days of delivery. Contact Dee at dee@juplifestudio.com with your order reference to arrange a return before sending an item back. For a damaged item or an issue with your order, include a description and photos so we can help. Customers pay return postage for unused-item returns. The studio covers return postage for damaged or incorrect orders; contact Dee first so we can arrange it. Once your return arrives and is inspected, we’ll confirm the refund with you and return approved amounts to your original payment method. Your bank or payment provider determines when the credit appears. TPU pieces: dishwasher safe on a normal cycle (no high-heat). PLA/PETG: hand wash.
