Many coaster printers have you approve a digital proof, then print. We print a free sample of your own design and mail it to you first, and your batch ships within 10 days of your approval. Here is every step, and what to have ready at each one.

1. Send your art

A logo file is enough to start: PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF, up to 4 MB, through the sample form. A clean vector file (SVG or PDF) gives the sharpest edges. Your logo isn't printed as ink on the surface; we bake it into the coaster as a raised relief, so bold shapes and clear outlines read best.

2. Pick your colors

Our colorways are named after ingredients, like pumpkin, coconut, peach, sage and key lime, and they change with the season's batch. Tell us your brand colors and we'll tell you what fits before we print the sample.

3. Approve a printed sample, not just a screen proof

This is the step that differs most from paper coaster printers. Many approve a design on screen: Coaster Factory, for example, says it "will create a digital proof and send it to you for your approval" (Coaster Factory FAQ, as of November 2026). A screen proof shows the artwork. It can't show how a raised logo feels in your hand or how a color reads in your room.

We print your actual design and mail it to you, free. You hold it, set a glass on it and flex it before you commit to a batch.

4. We bake your batch

Your batch prints in our Jupiter studio on a fleet of 30 printers (10 Bambu P1S, 10 Qidi Max 4 and 10 Prusa Core One) in QIDI TECHNOLOGY TPU 95A High Flow, a flexible filament the maker describes as high-elastic, shock absorbent and wear-resistant. Once you approve the sample, it ships within 10 days. U.S. shipping is free, and we ship anywhere in the country.

To be fair about speed: Coaster Factory says its Value Pack coasters are "normally ready to ship approximately 2 weeks from proof approval" and its Short Run coasters ship in about 3 to 5 business days from approval (Coaster Factory FAQ, as of November 2026). If you need paper coasters in days, a short-run paper printer is faster. Our extra step is a free printed sample of your design, in your hands before the batch.
