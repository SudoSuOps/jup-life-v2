The rule of thumb

The common guide is one coaster per guest plus a 10 to 15 percent cushion "for spares, replacements and guests who want to keep one as a memento," leaning toward the higher end for weddings, where coasters get swapped often (4OVER4, as of November 2026). Not every guest takes one home, so one per guest plus a little is plenty.

When to order

Count back from the date: request a free sample of your design, approve it, and your batch ships within 10 days of approval, plus carrier transit. Leave room for a second sample if you tweak the names or monogram. Here's how the whole process works.

What goes on them

You send the art: your names, initials, a monogram, the date. We bake it in as a raised relief in colors named after ingredients like peach, sage, cream and ruby. They're washable and flexible, so they get used after the wedding.
