How an order works

How custom logo coasters are made, step by step:

  1. Request a free sample. Tell us where to send it.
  2. Send your art. PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF, up to 4 MB.
  3. Approve a sample print. We bake your design into a sample coaster as a raised relief, for your approval.
  4. We bake your batch. It ships within 10 days of your approval. U.S. shipping is free.

Good questions.

{% block faq %}{% endblock %}
Is there a minimum?

Logo batches start at 200 coasters: 200 at $2.00 each ($400), 500 at $1.90 ($950), 1,000 at $1.75 ($1,750).

How long does a batch take?

Your batch ships within 10 days of approving the sample print. Carrier transit time is on top of that.

Do you ship outside Florida?

Yes. We print in Jupiter, FL and ship anywhere in the U.S., and U.S. shipping is always free.

What are they made of?

TPU 95A, a flexible printed filament. It bends instead of breaking, so a dropped stack is just a stack to pick up.

Can I wash them?

Yes. They're washable. Rinse and repeat.
