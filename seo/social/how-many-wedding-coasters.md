How many coasters do you need for a wedding?

The rule of thumb: one per guest, plus a 10 to 15 percent cushion for spares and the guests who keep one. For 150 guests that's 165 to 173. A 200-coaster batch covers it, with names, a monogram or the date baked in.

The full table for 100 to 300 guests, and how to time the order: https://juplifestudio.com/blog/how-many-wedding-coasters
