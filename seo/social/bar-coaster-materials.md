Which coaster material should a bar buy? It depends on whether you'd rather reorder or wash.

Pulpboard absorbs best and costs least, but it's single-use. Cork and rubber are reusable, and the vendor guides don't even agree on whether cork absorbs. Our TPU 95A coasters don't absorb at all: condensation beads on top. They rinse clean, bend instead of breaking, and carry your logo as a raised relief.

We wrote up the tradeoffs, including where paper is the better choice: https://juplifestudio.com/blog/bar-coaster-materials
